# License inventory

`../LICENSE` applies to first-party source only. Third-party notices already
present in imported snapshots are retained at their original paths. The
machine-readable inventory records explicit exceptions and unresolved items.
It is a publication checklist, not legal clearance.

Before a public release:

1. Confirm first-party ownership and the chosen license with the rights holder.
2. Resolve restricted/unlicensed third-party redistribution or exclude it from
   the public source archive and images; keep a downloader/adapter when allowed.
3. Retain original license texts for exact dependency revisions and provide
   corresponding source wherever their terms require it.
4. Assign and record separate terms for checkpoints, input data and source data.

The asset manager verifies content identity. It does not grant usage or
redistribution rights and does not silently change model license terms.
